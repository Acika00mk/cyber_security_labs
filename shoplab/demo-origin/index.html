Cross-site request forgery (Lab 3)

Log in to ShopLab as Alice, then submit this form. It posts to ShopLab while you are on this page. If the shop has no CSRF protection on reorder, Alice gets a new paid order she did not intend.

Note: localhost:4000 and localhost:3000 are treated as the same site in modern browsers, so your session cookie is still sent — similar to attacks across subdomains in production.
